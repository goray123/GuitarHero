# Q: 전체적으로 보스패턴에 몸통 판정이 어떻게 들어가있는지 설명해줘

## Answer

Expanded from original query via graph vocab: [torso, hitbox, rush, leap, rear, barrage, backstep]. Current working tree and Boss1 prefab were checked after graph traversal. Boss body/player physical collision is ignored in Boss1ProximityAttack.FixedUpdate (L152-L160), so body overlap is not a permanent contact attack. Shared torso is a rectangle derived from the root BoxCollider2D, with prefab torsoCoverage=0.5 (Boss1.prefab L245), selecting the attack-facing half across the full body height. Boss1AttackHitboxes.GetTorsoArea (L122-L133) clips only punch attack 2 bottom to SlideHeight+0.1H; torso top remains body top. Melee Evaluate (L63-L90) enables torso only during Active: sweep 0.15s 6 damage, punch 0.15s 6, slam 0.20s 10; main hit and torso share per-strike hitPlayers. Rush uses a separate crouched attack rectangle built from covered torso width plus 0.25H arm reach and 0.9H height, enabled only in Moving, and sweeps its travelled distance (Boss1BoostRush L140-L160, L189-L211); 10 damage once per rush. Leap HitLanding (L148-L154) applies forward torso 10 in Impact; AdvanceWaves (L159-L173) also applies forward torso 6 while waves remain. Landing, both waves, and torso share one hitPlayers set for the whole leap. Rear missile explosions call HitTorso(-Facing), so rear half deals 6 during explosions (Boss1RearMissiles L136-L150), sharing the first explosion hit set. Aimed missile explosions call HitTorso by Direction.x and deal 6 (Boss1AimedMissile L150-L160); aim/flying do not. These two torso checks have no condition relating explosion distance to boss body, so a player inside the relevant torso half can be hit even outside the explosion circle. Current barrage source Boss1MissileBarrage.AdvanceRockets (L97-L134) contains explosion circles only and no HitTorso call; the graph edge claiming a barrage torso call is stale. Backstep/approach movement have no own torso attack; independently active aimed missile explosions can still apply torso damage. PlayerHealth.TryTakeDamage L55-L67 gives default 1 second invulnerability after an accepted hit. Damage fractions use serialized referencePlayerMaxHealth=100, not the current PlayerHealth.MaxHealth. No gameplay source edited and no Unity play test run.
